I Want to Be Adored

On a bus home to New Jersey for my girlfriend’s album release, Spotify played the Stone Roses’ “I Want to Be Adored.”

I recognized the name. I’d been exploring music connected to Manchester, following my interest in the Smiths. What stayed with me was the opening noise, the bassline, and the way the song gradually built.

There was something about the emotional tension and the eerie vocals that made me imagine what it would feel like to perform it.
